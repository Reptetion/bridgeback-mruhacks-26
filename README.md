# MRUHacks 2026 -- BridgeBack

## What is BridgeBack?
Bridgeback is an online marketplace platform that connects individuals to people with the proper skills to complete 
a project/task. It bridges the gap between individuals needing fast, short-term assistance to skilled individuals right on campus or in your local community.

## How Does It Work?
1. **2 Modes:** Toggle between "I Need Help" (Buyer Mode) and "I Want to Help" (Seller Mode) -- Note for demo purposes, the **Buyer Mode** is only displayed. 
2. **Click to Bridge:** Browse local requests or helpers presented as stackable cards displaying a calculated Match Score based on skill relevance location, and timeframe. Skip or click Bridge when you find a fit.
3. **Collaborate in the Bridge Room:** Enter a dedicated chat to finalize project details and complete payment processing.

## Connection To The Prompt - "REVITALIZE"
Unlike other online marketplace platforms like Fiverr, BridgeBack drwas inspiration from dating sites like Tinder to create a 
platform where finding someone is possible with just a swipe, creating a less saturated environment. Instead of a platform where
results are thrown right at you, <i>which can be quite overwhelming and difficult for new sellers to break into</i>, BridgeBack **bridges** that connection simply through a score, which is calculated based off relevance.
